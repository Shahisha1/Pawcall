Built for the whole crew

Bring your companions closer.

Whether you're heading home or exploring somewhere new, Pawcall makes it easier to regroup without changing how your pets move through the world.

01 / CALL

They come running

Eligible pets navigate toward your position using their normal pathfinding. They are not teleported to you.

02 / RANGE

Reach a little farther

Use the quick call nearby, or hold the Pawcall whistle to call companions from up to 48 blocks away.

03 / CARE

Let resting pets rest

Pets ordered to sit stay where they are. Only tamed animals owned by you respond to your call.

Two ways to call

One key. Two ranges.

The default keybind is G. You can change it in Minecraft's Controls menu.

Quick call / Any held item
12 blocks

Press G with an empty hand or any item. Your nearby eligible pets will start moving toward you.

Whistle call / Whistle in either hand
48 blocks

Hold the crafted Pawcall whistle in your main hand or offhand, then press G to extend your call range.

A few quick answers

Good to know.

Does it teleport my pets?

No. Pets use navigation to travel toward you. They still need a path they can follow.

Which pets respond?

Tamed animals owned by you, as long as they are not ordered to sit and are within range.

Can I use the whistle from my offhand?

Yes. The extended range activates when the Pawcall whistle is in either your main hand or offhand.

Can I change the keybind?

Yes. Open Options, then Controls, then Key Binds. Find the Pawcall binding and assign another key if needed.
