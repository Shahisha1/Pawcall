Field notes / 02

Three things happen after the call.

Pawcall keeps the familiar rhythm of Minecraft. It gives your companions a nudge, then lets the world do the rest.

01 — Send the signal
⌁

Press G

Make a quick call from wherever you are. No whistle required for the shorter range.

02 — The right pets respond
↝

They hear you

Your owned, tamed pets in range start heading over. Pets ordered to sit are left alone.

03 — Find your way back
⌂

They catch up

They use their normal navigation to reach you. No instant jumps, no shortcuts through the world.

Workbench notes / 04

Two simple ingredients. One very useful sound.

Make the Pawcall whistle with an iron ingot above a bone in a crafting grid. It also appears in the Tools & Utilities creative tab. Right-click it to call your pets directly.

Trail questions / 06

Before you head out.

Do my pets teleport to me?

No. Pawcall tells eligible pets to navigate toward you using their normal movement.

Which pets respond to the call?

Only tamed animals owned by the player who made the call, and only if they are not ordered to sit.

Does the whistle have to be in my main hand?

No. Holding it in either your main hand or offhand enables the extended 48-block range.

Can I use the whistle without pressing G?

Yes. Right-clicking the Pawcall whistle also triggers a call.

Can I change the keybind?

Yes. Find Pawcall under Options → Controls → Key Binds and assign another key.

Is the mod open source?

Yes. Pawcall is available on GitHub under the MIT License.
