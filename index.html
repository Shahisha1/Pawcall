Ranges are measured from the player. Calls share a one-second cooldown. The whistle range is capped at 128 blocks if adjusted in the source.

What happens after you call?

Calls select your owned, tamed TamableAnimal pets in your current dimension. Sitting pets are left alone.

They walk to you

Pets use their normal navigation; Pawcall does not teleport them.

Pathfinding still applies

A call is a movement request, not guaranteed retrieval. Walls, terrain, hazards or an unreachable route can stop a pet from reaching you. Pets must be in the same dimension and inside the selected range when the call is made.

Change the key if needed

G is the default keybind. Reassign it in Options → Controls → Key Binds. Right-clicking the whistle also triggers a call.

Craft the whistle.

Use a crafting grid. Place one iron ingot in the first slot of the top row, then one bone directly below it. The remaining slots stay empty.

Pattern: top row — iron ingot; middle row — bone; bottom row — empty.

Also available in the Tools & Utilities creative tab.

  4. Launch your Fabric profile.

Things to know.

Which animals are supported?

The implementation queries Minecraft's TamableAnimal type and checks ownership. Wolves and cats are examples. It does not simply call every animal in the world; other mobs are included only if they use that entity type and ownership behaviour.

Why didn't a pet respond?

The pet must be tamed, owned by you, within range, in the same dimension, and not ordered to sit. It also needs a usable path through the loaded world. Pawcall never teleports pets or force-loads distant chunks.

Can I call pets repeatedly?

Calls share a one-second cooldown. The mod also avoids resetting a pet's navigation when it already has an active path.

What does the whistle change?

Hold the Pawcall whistle in either hand and press G, or right-click it, to use the extended 96-block range. Without the whistle, the quick call reaches 12 blocks.
