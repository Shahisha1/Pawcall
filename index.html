Under the hood

What the G key actually does

No magic. Here is the whole sequence, straight from the mod's code.

  1. 01

    Finds your pets

    Scans a box around you for tamed animals that you own, out to 12 or 48 blocks. Other players' pets are ignored.

  2. 02

    Skips the sitters

    Any pet you told to sit stays exactly where it is. Your wolf on guard at the base keeps guarding it.

  3. 03

    Sends them to you

    Each remaining pet gets a path to your position and walks it at 1.25× speed. If a pet has no route, it won't arrive. Pawcall never forces it.

  4. 04

    Tells you how it went

    You hear a short chime and get a chat line with the pet count. If nobody was in range, it says so.

FAQ

Things people ask first

Do pets teleport to me?

No. They walk, so they need a route. A pet on the far side of a ravine or locked in a fenced pen won't make it.

Which pets answer?

Tamed animals you own that aren't sitting and are within range.

Can I use the whistle from my offhand?

Yes. Either hand extends the range to 48 blocks.

Can I change the key?

Yes: Options, Controls, Key Binds, then look for Call Pets under Pawcall.

Does it work on servers?

Install it on the client and the server. The server handles the call, and the client sends the keypress.

Why a cooldown?

One second between calls stops key-mashing from spamming the server with path requests.
