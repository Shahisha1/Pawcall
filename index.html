Call your tamed pets in Minecraft.

Pawcall sends nearby pets you own running toward you. Press G for a 12-block call, or hold the whistle to reach 48 blocks. They use normal pathfinding—no teleporting.

02 / PET BEHAVIOUR

What happens after you call?

Pawcall finds nearby TamableAnimal entities that are tamed and owned by the player who called. Pets ordered to sit are excluded.

A

They walk to you

The mod sends pets toward the player's position using their normal navigation at movement speed 1.25. It does not teleport them.

B

Only your pets respond

Other players' pets, untamed animals and pets told to sit are not included. The current implementation targets Minecraft's TamableAnimal entity type.

C

Pathfinding still applies

A call is a movement request, not guaranteed retrieval. Walls, terrain, hazards or an unreachable route can stop a pet from reaching you. Pets must be in the same dimension and inside the selected range when the call is made.

D

Change the key if needed

G is the default keybind. Reassign it in Options → Controls → Key Binds. Right-clicking the whistle also triggers a call.

03 / CRAFTING RECIPE

Craft the whistle.

Use a crafting grid. Place one iron ingot in the first slot of the top row, then one bone directly below it. The remaining slots stay empty.

Pattern: top row — iron ingot; middle row — bone; bottom row — empty.

The whistle is also available in the Tools & Utilities creative tab. Right-click it to call pets without pressing G.

05 / LIMITATIONS

Things to know.

Will every pet always reach me?

No. Pawcall asks the pet's navigation system to move toward you. It cannot guarantee arrival if the route is blocked, unreachable, or interrupted by the game world.

Which animals are supported?

The implementation queries Minecraft's TamableAnimal type and checks ownership. Wolves and cats are examples. It does not simply call every animal in the world; other mobs are included only if they use that entity type and ownership behaviour.

Can I call pets in another dimension?

No. The search runs in the player's current server level, so pets in another dimension are not part of that call.
